Publications

Q-VGM: Q-Guided Value-Gradient Matching for Offline-to-Online RL of Flow-Matching VLA Policies
Ziqian Wang, Jiayu Sun, Xingjian Mao, Minqian Wang, Yao Mu
arXiv preprint, 2026 · under review

An offline-to-online RL method for fine-tuning flow-matching VLA policies with a learned critic. Applying critic gradients to a flow policy normally means backpropagating through the multi-step denoising process, which is costly and unstable at VLA scale. Motivated by an optimal-control view of denoising, Q-VGM turns clean-action value gradients into local velocity corrections at late denoising steps and fine-tunes the pretrained action expert by matching these critic-guided targets, with gradients flowing only through local velocity predictions. On LIBERO it lifts a few-shot-SFT π0.5 policy from 84.6% to 90.7% offline and to 98.4% after online training, with 5.3× the sample efficiency of on-policy RL; on three real-world bimanual tasks, offline Q-VGM raises average success from 66.7% to 98.3%.

Scene2Gym: Transforming Real-World Scenes into Interactive Training Environments for Robot Learning
Ziheng Ji, Fengyi Chen, Ziqian Wang, Zhitao Wang, Feng Qiu, Wenguo Hou, Ming Yang, Tong Qin
IROS 2026

A framework that turns real-world scene observations into interactive, metric-consistent digital twins for embodied policy learning. It combines 3D Gaussian Splatting for photorealistic rendering with an object-centric metric-alignment pipeline that anchors reconstructed assets to real-world scale, and a hybrid co-simulation stack that decouples rendering from the physics clock for closed-loop training. Imitation-learning policies trained purely on Scene2Gym-synthesized data match policies trained on real human demonstrations, articulated-object manipulation transfers zero-shot to a real microwave-heating task, and visual RL policies reach a 56% zero-shot real-world success rate.
