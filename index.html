Publications

Q-VGM: Q-Guided Value-Gradient Matching for Offline-to-Online RL of Flow-Matching VLA
Ziqian Wang, R. Zhang, Y. Liu, Xingjian Mao, Minqian Wang, Yao Mu†
Under Review, 2026
pdf | | arXiv

An offline-to-online RL framework for fine-tuning flow-matching VLAs (e.g., π0.5) that converts critic action-gradients into velocity-field supervision, avoiding the unstable backpropagation through the denoising chain required by standard actor-critic updates. On three real-robot bimanual manipulation tasks, Q-VGM raises average success from 66.7% to 98.3%. On LIBERO, it improves a few-shot SFT policy from 86.9% to 93.0% with only 150 offline rollout episodes per task, and to 99.7% with offline-to-online training, matching PPO with ~6× fewer rollout episodes.

Scene2Gym: Transforming Real-World Scenes into Interactive Training Environments for Robot Learning
Ziheng Ji*, Fengyi Chen*, Ziqian Wang, Zhitao Wang, Feng Qiu, Wenguo Hou, Ming Yang, Tong Qin
IROS 2026

A framework that turns real-world scenes into interactive, metric-consistent 3D Gaussian Splatting digital twins for robot learning. I led the RL and imitation-learning policy training, where visual RL policies trained in Scene2Gym achieve a 56% zero-shot sim-to-real success rate.
