I am an undergraduate in Computer Science at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor (Fall 2026). Previously I studied Electrical and Computer Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I am interested in building embodied agents that interact with the physical world — in particular, how reinforcement learning, foundation models, and world models can be combined to build generalist robots. Currently, I am a research intern at AgiBot, scaling off-policy RL fine-tuning to a real-robot fleet, and at ScaleLab (SJTU) with Prof. Yao Mu, where I work on RL fine-tuning for vision-language-action policies.
